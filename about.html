Our story

VAA started with three school friends — Vikas, Ayush, and Ashish — who shared classrooms, lunch breaks, and big dreams. We studied together, grew up together, and somewhere between exams and holidays, we made a promise: one day, we'll build something of our own.

After finishing 12th standard, we looked around and saw the same problem everywhere. Workers struggled to find steady work. Customers struggled to find reliable workers. Materials took too long to reach sites. Tools, supplies, skilled help — everything was scattered across different places. Nothing was broken, but nothing was connected.

We searched. We looked at how things worked in other cities, other states. And we realised — there is no single platform in India where everything is accessible from one site. Not for labour. Not for materials. Not for tools. Not for skilled professionals. You always need to call 5 different people to get one job done.

That's when we decided to build it ourselves. In 2026, we launched VAA — a platform with one simple belief: if you can imagine it, you should be able to get it from one place.

"We're not just building a business. We're building something that doesn't exist in India yet — everything you need, from one site."

The name VAA is formed from the first letters of our names — a small reminder of who we are and why we started. Today we serve customers, workers, and suppliers across Karnal and beyond. We're still at the beginning — but we're building every day, together.
